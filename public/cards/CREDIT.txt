Card faces are crops of Johann Kaspar Hechtel, Das Spiel der Hofnung (The Game of Hope), Nuremberg, 1799.
Scan: Wikimedia Commons, public domain (artwork published before 1931).
Source sheet: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Das_Spiel_der_Hofnung_(The_Game_of_Hope).png
Original object: British Museum 1896,0501.495.

Card back (cards/back.jpg) is an xAI Imagine photograph of a worn oxblood
and gilt damask reverse, meant to sit on green baize with the 1799 faces.

Table scenes (table/seat.jpg, table/pit.jpg) are overhead photographs of a
wooden table and a green card mat. The pack is laid out on that mat in the
picture. Extra cloths and backs live in table/cloth-*.jpg and backs/.
